A maid, cook or nanny often spends more hours in your home than some family members. They see your children, your elderly parents, your keys and your valuables. Most domestic workers are honest and hardworking, and a simple verification protects them too: it gives them a clear record of who they are and that they were checked fairly.

## Why verify domestic help

- **Safety of children and elders**, who are most often alone with the worker.
- **Identity mix-ups:** the person who comes to work is sometimes not the person you spoke to or the one an agency introduced.
- **Peace of mind** if something goes missing or a dispute arises: you have a record made at the start.
- **Police requests:** in many cities the police encourage families to share details of domestic workers.

## What to check

1. **Identity:** Aadhaar and, if possible, PAN or Voter ID, matched to the person.
2. **Face match and liveness:** confirms the worker is real and present, not a photo of someone else.
3. **Home address and native place:** where they live now and where their family lives.
4. **Past work:** which homes they worked in and why they left.
5. **Declarations:** any police or court case.
6. **A recorded statement:** the worker's own words, with date, time and location.

## How to do it respectfully

Tell the worker before you start: you verify everyone who works in your home, it takes a few minutes, and it is done on their own phone. Ask for consent. Do not keep loose photocopies of their ID lying around; a proper check stores only what is needed and keeps it safe.
