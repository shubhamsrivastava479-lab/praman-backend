An NRI match can look ideal: a good job overseas, a stable life, a big wedding to plan. But distance also makes it easier to hide facts, and families in India often cannot meet the person's friends, colleagues or neighbours. A careful, respectful verification before the engagement helps.

## Why NRI matches need extra care

- **Hidden previous marriage** or unresolved divorce abroad
- **Uncertain immigration status:** a visa that does not allow long stay or work
- **Exaggerated job and income**
- **Fake or borrowed profile** photos
- **Pressure to marry in haste** during a short visit to India
- **Abandonment after marriage,** leaving a spouse in India without support

## What to verify

1. **Identity:** passport, and the name on every document must match. Check details such as passport number, validity and visa or work permit where the person is willing to share.
2. **Marital status:** documents showing divorce or death of a previous spouse if the person was married before, and details of any children.
3. **Job and employer:** the employer, role and tenure. Verify through the employer's official contact, not a number the match gives you.
4. **Income:** pay slips or tax documents shown on camera, with the person's consent.
5. **Immigration status:** the visa type and how long it is valid. Ask what it allows for a spouse.
6. **Family and address:** parents, siblings and the home address in India.
7. **Overseas address:** where they live now and with whom.

## How to do it across time zones

1. **Meet on video** several times, including with family members present.
2. **Ask for the same verification from both sides,** so it is mutual.
3. **Verify independently:** do not rely on documents sent by the person alone.
4. **Visit if possible** before final decisions, or ask a trusted relative to meet.
5. **Take legal advice** about registering the marriage and your rights.

## Warning signs

- Reluctance to be on live video, or to show documents on camera
- Stories that change between conversations
- A request for money, gifts or "customs charges"
- Urgency to complete the marriage during a short visit
