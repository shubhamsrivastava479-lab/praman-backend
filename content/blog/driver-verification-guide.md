A driver is trusted with a vehicle, your passengers and often your schedule and your family's whereabouts. Whether you hire one for home, run a cab business or manage a fleet, a licence alone is not a check.

## Why driver verification matters

- **Safety:** the person at the wheel carries the lives of others.
- **Fake or borrowed documents:** a licence that belongs to someone else.
- **Wrong vehicle class:** a licence that does not cover the vehicle he will drive.
- **Misuse of vehicles and fuel**, and goods or vehicles that go missing.
- **Accountability:** if an accident occurs, you need to know who he really is.

## What to check

1. **Licence validity:** is it active and not expired?
2. **Vehicle classes:** does it cover a car, a commercial vehicle or a heavy vehicle, as your job needs?
3. **Identity:** Aadhaar or PAN, matched to the person with a face match.
4. **Address:** where he lives now and where his family lives.
5. **Past employers:** where he drove before, for how long and why he left. Call one of them.
6. **Court record check** for roles of high trust.
7. **Recorded statement** with the date on record.

## Red flags

- A licence photo that does not look like him
- Reluctance to share a home address or family contact
- Gaps in his work history that he cannot explain
- Several short jobs, each ending abruptly

## Test the skill separately

Documents show that a person is allowed to drive. A short road test, done by you or someone you trust, shows whether he drives safely. Do both.
