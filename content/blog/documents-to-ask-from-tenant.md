A rent agreement is only as good as the person who signs it. Before you hand over the keys, ask for a small set of documents that tell you who the tenant is, where they work and whether they can pay. Here is a practical list and what each paper really proves.

## Core documents

**1. Photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, passport or driving licence).**
It shows a name and a photo. It does not prove that the person in front of you is the one on the card, so match the face to the photo.

**2. Address proof.**
It shows where the person lived earlier or where they are from. For a tenant who is moving in from another city, ask for the permanent address as well.

**3. Employment proof.**
An office ID card, offer letter or recent payslips show where the tenant works. For students, a college ID and a guarantor's details are common.

**4. Income proof.**
Payslips or bank statements indicate whether the rent fits their income. As a general rule, rent should fit comfortably within a tenant's monthly income.

**5. Previous landlord reference.**
A call to the previous landlord is one of the most useful checks. Ask about rent payments, upkeep and why the tenant left.

**6. Passport-size photos and details of all adults who will live there.**
Know exactly who is moving in, not only who signs.

## Extra for special cases

- **Foreign nationals:** passport, visa and any registration required by local rules.
- **Companies renting for staff:** company registration, authorised signatory's ID and a list of occupants.
- **Students:** guardian's ID and contact details.

## Be careful with documents

- **Ask only for what you need.** More papers do not make a safer deal.
- **Store them securely** and delete them when you no longer need them.
- **Never accept documents sent through a stranger.**
- **Beware of edited papers.** Payslips and bank statements can be altered. A direct check is safer.

## Confirm the person matches the papers

Papers prove little unless the person is verified. A short consent-based video call, where the tenant shows their ID and completes a face match, closes that gap.
