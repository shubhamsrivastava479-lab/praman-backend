PGs and hostels have many residents, frequent changes and shared spaces. That makes it easy for a problem person to slip in. A short, standard verification for every resident keeps the place safe for everyone.

## Why it matters

- Safety of other residents, especially in women's and student housing
- Protection from rent defaults and damage
- Compliance with local police rules
- A clear record if something goes wrong

## A quick routine for every resident

1. **Government photo ID:** collect a copy with consent and match the face with the person.
2. **Recent photograph.**
3. **Permanent address** and a guardian's phone number.
4. **College or employer proof:** ID card or letter.
5. **Emergency contact.**
6. **Previous place of stay** and, if possible, a contact there.
7. **Signed house rules** and a rent agreement.
8. **Police intimation or verification** as per local requirement.

## Make it fast

- Collect details on a form or a WhatsApp link before the person arrives
- Check names across documents
- Verify by video call for people who join from other cities
- Keep a simple register with join and exit dates

## Red flags

- ID that looks edited or does not match the person
- Reluctance to share a guardian's contact
- No clear reason for the stay or no proof of study or work
- Wanting to pay a large advance in cash and move in at once

## Keep data safe

Store copies securely, share them only with authorities when required and delete them after a resident leaves, as your policy and local rules permit.

## Handling complaints

As a PG or hostel owner, keep a simple process for complaints, visitors and exits. Residents feel safer when rules are clear, and your verification record shows you took reasonable care if the police or a family ever asks questions.
