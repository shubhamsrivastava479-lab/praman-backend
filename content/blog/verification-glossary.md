Verification comes with a lot of short forms. Here is a plain-language glossary you can keep handy when you talk to vendors, auditors or your own team.

## Identity and onboarding

- **KYC (Know Your Customer):** confirming who a customer is before offering financial services.
- **KYB (Know Your Business):** the same for a company, including its owners and registration.
- **eKYC:** KYC done digitally, often with an ID number and an OTP.
- **Face match:** comparing a live face with the photo on an ID.
- **Liveness check:** confirming that a real person is present now.
- **OTP:** a one-time code sent to a phone to confirm access.

## Hiring and people checks

- **BGV (Background Verification):** checking education, past jobs, address, records and identity.
- **Address verification:** confirming that a person lives where they say.
- **Reference check:** speaking with a past employer or other contact.
- **Court or criminal record check:** searching public court records for cases.

## Insurance and claims

- **PIVC:** pre-issuance verification call before a policy starts.
- **PLVC:** post-login verification, done after a proposal is submitted.
- **Claim investigation:** a check of facts behind a claim.
- **TPA:** third-party administrator that handles health claims.
- **Cashless claim:** a claim paid directly to the hospital.

## Vehicles

- **RC:** registration certificate of a vehicle.
- **DL:** driving licence.
- **e-challan:** a traffic fine issued electronically.
- **FASTag:** electronic toll tag linked to a vehicle.

## Business

- **GST:** Goods and Services Tax registration.
- **CIN and DIN:** company and director identification numbers.
- **Udyam:** registration for micro, small and medium businesses.
- **Due diligence:** checking a business or person before a deal.

## Privacy

- **Consent:** permission to collect and use data for a stated purpose.
- **Data retention:** how long data is kept before it is deleted.
