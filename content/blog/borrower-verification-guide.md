Every loan decision depends on knowing who the borrower is, where they live and whether their income is real. Lenders traditionally send a field agent for residence and business verification. It works, but it is slow, expensive in smaller towns, and hard to audit later.

## Why borrower verification matters

- **Identity fraud:** loans taken in someone else's name.
- **Address fraud:** a borrower who cannot be found when repayments stop.
- **Inflated income:** a business that is smaller than declared, or a job that does not exist.
- **Agent shortcuts:** field reports filled without a real visit.

## What lenders check

1. **Identity:** PAN, Aadhaar and a face match.
2. **Residence:** the borrower at the stated address, the type of house, ownership or rent, family members.
3. **Business or employment:** the shop, office or factory, the nature of work, staff, stock; for salaried borrowers, the employer.
4. **Income:** bank statements, GST or ITR where relevant, and what the borrower says.
5. **Existing loans** and the purpose of the new one.
6. **References** from neighbours or business contacts where needed.

## Why field visits slow things down

Scheduling, travel and report writing take days. The borrower may not be home. The report is often a few lines and photos that are hard to check later.
