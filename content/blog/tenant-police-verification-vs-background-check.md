Many landlords use the two terms as if they mean the same thing. They do not. One is a formality you complete after the tenant is chosen. The other helps you choose the tenant in the first place.

## What police verification is

Police verification is the process of telling the local police who is living in your property. In many cities, landlords are expected to share the tenant's identity details with the police station, often through an online form. Its purpose is public safety and record keeping. The rules, forms and penalties differ from city to city, so check your own city's police website.

It usually does **not** tell you whether the tenant pays rent on time, whether the job and income they claimed are real, or whether their story about their previous home is true.

## What a tenant background check is

A background check is **your** due diligence before you hand over the keys. It answers practical questions:

- Is this person who they say they are? (ID and face match)
- Do they live and work where they claim?
- Is their income enough for the rent?
- Why did they leave their last home?
- Do they declare any court or police case?

## Why you need both

Police verification protects you if something goes wrong later, because the authorities have a record of who lived there. A background check reduces the chance that something goes wrong at all. Doing only the first means you may hand over a flat to a person you never really checked. Doing only the second can leave you out of step with local rules.

## How to do the background part quickly

1. Ask the tenant for their name and WhatsApp number and explain that you do a short check for every tenant.
2. Get their consent. A proper check is never done secretly.
3. Have the tenant show their ID and take a face and liveness check.
4. Ask about work, income, family members and previous address, and note whether the answers hold together.
5. Keep the record with your rent agreement.
