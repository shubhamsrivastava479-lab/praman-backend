Udyam registration is how many Indian micro, small and medium businesses identify themselves. Buyers, banks and marketplaces often ask for it. But a certificate on its own is only a document, and documents can be edited. Verifying it properly takes a few minutes.

## Why it matters

MSME status can affect payment terms, schemes, priority lending and supplier eligibility. If a business uses a copied or edited certificate, you may offer benefits to someone who does not qualify, or onboard a vendor who does not exist as described.

## What a registration usually shows

- The business name and type of organisation
- The registration number and date
- The owner or proprietor's name
- The business address and activity codes
- The declared category (micro, small or medium)

## How to verify

1. **Collect the Udyam number** and a copy of the certificate.
2. **Check the number on the government portal** and compare the name, address and category.
3. **Match with other documents:** PAN, GST and bank account should carry the same business or owner name.
4. **Check the person:** the one dealing with you should be the owner or an authorised person.
5. **Confirm the premises** for larger orders or loans.
6. **Keep a record** of the date and what you checked.

## What a registration does not show

- That the business is operating today
- Its turnover or profit
- That it pays its taxes or suppliers on time
- That the person you are talking to is its owner

## Why it helps both sides

For a genuine small business, a clean verification builds trust and speeds up orders and loans. For the buyer, it reduces the chance of paying an advance to a business that does not exist. A quick, consistent check for every vendor is better than a long check for a few.
