A plumber at the door, an electrician in the kitchen, a technician fixing the AC: home-service professionals come into your home, sometimes when you are alone. Platforms that send them, and households that hire them directly, both need confidence about who these people are.

## Why it matters

Customers trust the platform or the person they hire. One incident, a theft, an unsafe repair or harassment, damages that trust for a whole platform. Verification protects customers, and also protects honest professionals from suspicion.

## What to verify

1. **Identity:** government photo ID with a live face match.
2. **Address and contact:** where the person lives and a number of a family member.
3. **Skills:** certificates, training or references for the trade.
4. **Past work:** feedback from earlier customers or employers.
5. **Background:** court or police records where appropriate and with consent.
6. **Tools and safety:** proper equipment and basic safety knowledge, especially for electrical and gas work.
7. **Ongoing quality:** ratings, complaints and re-verification at intervals.

## For households

- Prefer verified platforms or trusted references
- Ask for ID and match it with the person arriving
- Share the technician's name and number with family
- Stay in the area where work is happening
- Get a bill and warranty for repairs

## For platforms

- Verify before the first job, not after
- Re-verify periodically and after complaints
- Make it easy for customers to report concerns
- Act fast on safety issues

## Red flags

- A different person from the one booked
- Refusing to show ID
- Pressure to buy parts from them or to pay in cash
- Asking personal questions about who lives at home

## Respect for professionals

Most electricians, plumbers and technicians are skilled people working hard. A simple, fast verification helps them win customers' trust and better jobs, so platforms should make it easy, quick and free of hassle for honest professionals.
