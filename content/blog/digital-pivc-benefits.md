A pre-issuance verification call (PIVC) is a simple idea: before a policy is issued, confirm with the customer that they know what they are buying. Done by traditional phone calls, it is slow, hard to audit and easy to game. Digital PIVC, done on a WhatsApp video link, fixes many of these weaknesses.

## Benefits for insurers

**1. Less mis-selling.** The customer hears the policy details and answers in their own words. Promises of guaranteed returns or "no premium after five years" come out on the record.

**2. The right person answers.** A face match and liveness check make it harder for an agent or relative to answer on the customer's behalf.

**3. Better evidence.** Video, timestamps and location are saved to the case. In a complaint, there is a record of what was said.

**4. Speed and scale.** Calls can be made in bulk, at any hour that suits customers, without tying up a call-centre queue.

**5. Fewer abandoned policies.** Customers who understand what they bought are less likely to cancel in the free-look period.

**6. Consistent questions.** Every customer hears the same questions in the same order, with flags for unclear answers.

## Benefits for customers

- A call at a **convenient time**, from their own phone
- Questions in **their own language**
- A clear chance to **say what they were told**, and to ask a person for help
- Protection from being sold the wrong product

## What to check when you adopt it

- Is consent for recording taken at the start?
- Can the customer reach a human?
- Are unclear answers escalated for review?
- Are recordings stored securely with access controls and logs?
- Does it fit your regulator's rules? Ask your compliance team.
