A warehouse needs two hundred pickers by next week. A security agency has to deploy guards across ten sites. In blue-collar hiring, verification often becomes the bottleneck, or gets skipped under pressure. A clear, repeatable process lets you hire fast without taking blind risks.

## Why bulk hiring is hard

- Large numbers arrive at once
- Many workers have limited paperwork or digital habits
- Names differ across documents
- Supervisors are busy and checks are inconsistent
- Dropouts and no-shows waste effort

## A process that scales

1. **Set the minimum checks** for each role and keep them the same for everyone.
2. **Collect details in a sheet:** name, phone, ID type and number, role and site.
3. **Reach out on WhatsApp,** where the worker already is, with a short link and clear instructions.
4. **Verify ID and face** on a quick video step.
5. **Review only the flagged cases:** mismatches, unclear photos, duplicate IDs.
6. **Release the rest** to onboarding in batches.
7. **Keep records** by site and date, so audits are easy.

## Tips that save time

- Tell workers in advance what to keep ready
- Give simple instructions in their language
- Run checks in the evening or near the site when workers are free
- Track status daily and chase the pending ones
- Avoid collecting more than you need

## Watch for

- The same ID used by several people
- A different person appearing than the ID photo
- Workers asking someone else to complete the step

## Common mistakes

- Starting work before any check is complete
- Letting each supervisor use a different method
- Collecting documents on paper that nobody can search later
- Treating every mismatch as fraud instead of asking first

A simple rule, a shared sheet and a daily status review remove most delays and give you records you can show a client or auditor.
