Gig platforms pay thousands of partners every week. When a payout goes to the wrong account, it is lost money and a support headache. When it goes to a rented or fake account, it can also be a sign of fraud. A simple check of the name on the bank account or UPI ID before the first payout closes that gap.

## Why it matters

- **Failed payouts:** wrong account numbers lead to returns and complaints.
- **Rented accounts:** a verified partner's profile used by someone else.
- **Fake partners:** payouts routed to accounts unrelated to the person.
- **Fraud rings:** several partners paid into the same account.
- **Audit and compliance:** knowing where money goes.

## How to do it

1. **Collect the account or UPI details** at onboarding, with the partner's consent.
2. **Check the registered name** of the account holder against the name on the partner's ID.
3. **Allow small differences,** such as initials or order of names, and review bigger ones.
4. **Flag shared accounts:** the same account used by many partners.
5. **Re-check when details change,** especially just before large payouts.
6. **Give partners a simple way** to fix mistakes.

## Handling mismatches fairly

- Ask the partner to explain, and request a proof such as a passbook page
- Accept close family accounts only with a documented reason
- Keep records of decisions

## Good practice

- Pay only after the first verification is complete
- Limit changes of payout accounts to a verified process
- Alert partners on changes, so they notice misuse
- Protect account data and share it only where needed

## Communicate clearly

Tell partners at onboarding that payouts go only to an account in their own name, and explain how to fix a mismatch. Clear rules reduce support calls and discourage account renting before it starts.
