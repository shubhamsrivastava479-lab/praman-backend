A pre-issuance verification call is the last point at which an insurer can check that the customer truly understands what is being bought. When it is rushed or scripted, mis-selling slips through and shows up later as complaints, cancellations and disputes. Good questions make the difference.

## Why mis-selling happens

- Targets push the agent to close quickly
- Returns, bonuses or 'one-time' premiums are described loosely
- Lock-in periods and charges are not mentioned
- Policies are sold to people who cannot afford the premium
- Someone other than the customer answers on the call

## The questions every call should ask

1. **Identity:** please confirm your full name and date of birth.
2. **Purpose:** in your own words, what product did you buy and why?
3. **Premium:** how much will you pay, how often, and for how many years?
4. **Lock-in and benefits:** do you understand when you can withdraw and what you will receive?
5. **Risk:** do you understand whether returns are guaranteed or linked to markets?
6. **Source of the idea:** did anyone promise returns, bonus or a free gift to buy this?
7. **Affordability:** can you comfortably pay this premium every year?
8. **Documents:** have you received the policy documents and the free-look period details?
9. **Consent:** do you confirm that you took this decision yourself?

## How to run the call well

- Ask open questions first and let the customer answer, rather than reading 'yes' for them
- Use the customer's own language
- Listen for hesitation, someone prompting in the background or unclear answers
- Never complete the call if the customer says they were promised something that is not in the policy
