Deadlines and customer reminders

Every new case gets a deadline. For AI-call cases the customer gets WhatsApp reminders with the call link until the call is done. Cases past their deadline are marked late on your dashboard.
The template must be approved by Meta, with one variable: the link. You can already use the same template as for the first message.
When set, every link message sent outside WhatsApp's 24-hour window uses this template instead: {{1}} customer name, {{2}} reason (for example "checking your health insurance claim (claim no. HC/26/88123)"), {{3}} the video call link (plus the Android app link for live calls). Leave empty to keep the 1-variable template above.

Review, alerts and reports

When this is on, even a call the AI marks "Verified" shows as Awaiting review until someone opens the report and gives the final verdict. Turn it off only if you want clean calls to complete by themselves.

Your checklist — what the AI asks and collects

Choose a service, then decide which standard questions are asked, add your own questions, and mark the items that must be done. If a mandatory item is missing, the call can never end as "Verified" and a live call is recommended. Identity steps (name, confirmations, face, eye blink, Aadhaar, PAN) cannot be switched off. Changes apply to calls that start after you save.

What we send, and how to check it is from us
      
A POST with JSON like {"id","event","createdAt","data":{"caseId","caseRef","claimNo","status","aiCall":{"decision","summary","flags","checks":[…]}, "review":…}}. Answer with any 2xx status within 10 seconds. The header X-Praman-Signature: t=…,v1=… is an HMAC-SHA256 of t + "." + body with your signing secret — check it before trusting the message, and ignore messages older than 5 minutes. The header X-Praman-Delivery is unique per message, so you can ignore repeats.


const crypto = require('crypto');
function ok(secret, rawBody, header) {
  const m = /t=(\d+),v1=([a-f0-9]+)/.exec(header || '');
  if (!m || Math.abs(Date.now()/1000 - m[1]) > 300) return false;
  const want = crypto.createHmac('sha256', secret).update(m[1] + '.' + rawBody).digest('hex');
  return crypto.timingSafeEqual(Buffer.from(want), Buffer.from(m[2]));
}

Data retention

    

    
After the period ends, only the stored files (photos, recordings, documents) of finished cases are deleted. The case, the AI report and the full record of what happened (chain of custody) stay. To keep the files of one case for ever (court case, complaint), open the case and use Legal hold. To change the period, contact Praman.
