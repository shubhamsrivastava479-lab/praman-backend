Features

Everything a verification case needs, in one place

From the first message to the final report, each step is recorded, time-stamped and easy to show to a reviewer.

💬

WhatsApp link, no app needed

The customer gets a personal link with their name and the reason for the call. They join from their phone browser.

🎥

Live video with an executive

A trained executive can verify face, documents and surroundings on a live call, with consent, location and recording.

🤖

Automated AI call

Questions are asked in the customer's own language. Answers are written down and checked against the record you hold.

📱

Screen sharing from Android

Our Android app lets the customer share their screen when the case needs it, for example to show a message or a document.

📍

Location and time proof

Each call stores where and when it took place, who was present and what was agreed.

📄

Clear evidence report

A first page shows the key findings at a glance. The rest lists checks, answers, photos and documents, with a fingerprint so changes can be spotted.

🔎

Fraud and consistency checks

Fixed rules look at edited PDFs, photo details, repeated numbers, vehicles, policies, hospitals and garages across cases.

⏱️

Deadlines and reminders

Every case has a due time. Customers get WhatsApp reminders, and late cases are flagged for your team.

🏢

Built for teams and partners

Separate access for each client, roles for agents and viewers, an insurer view, bulk upload from Excel and an API for your own systems.

Instant checks

ID, vehicle, business, bank and court checks in seconds

Along with the call, Praman can confirm a person, a vehicle, a business or a bank account. Checks run with consent and every result is saved with the case report.

🪪

Identity documents

  • PAN check: name, date of birth and Aadhaar link status
  • Aadhaar through DigiLocker (masked number only)
  • Voter ID check
  • Driving licence check: validity and vehicle classes
🙂

Face, liveness and deepfake

  • Face match between the selfie and the ID photo
  • Liveness check from a selfie
  • Deepfake check for photos
  • Deepfake check for voice recordings
📱

Mobile number and digital checks

  • Name linked to a mobile number
  • PAN linked to a mobile number
  • WhatsApp profile check (business or personal, profile photo)
  • Digital footprint: online accounts linked to the number
🚗

Vehicle checks

  • RC details: owner, make, model, fuel, insurance and fitness
  • Vehicle number from the chassis number
  • Pending e-challans (traffic fines)
  • FASTag status and history
💼

Employment and income checks

  • UAN from PAN or mobile number
  • Employment history from EPFO: employer, joining and leaving dates
  • Employer (establishment) check
  • Read a salary slip: company, designation, pay
🏢

Business checks

  • GST number details, and GST numbers held by a PAN
  • Udyam (MSME) registration
  • Company (CIN) and director (DIN) details
  • TAN check
🏦

Bank, payment and credit checks

  • Bank account holder name (penny drop)
  • UPI ID holder name
  • IFSC and branch details
  • Read a cheque or bank statement
⚖️

Court record check

  • Civil and criminal court records by name, father’s name and address
📍

Address and location

  • Address to map location, and map location to address
  • Distance between two places (DIGIPIN)
  • Electricity bill details as address proof
  • Live location and time on every call
✍️

Aadhaar eSign

  • Consent forms and declarations signed with Aadhaar OTP

How it works

From case to report in four steps

No visit is needed for most cases. Field work is added only where it is really needed.

Add the case

Enter it, or upload many from Excel or the API.

Customer gets the link

One WhatsApp message with the reason and the call link.

Verification call

Identity, answers, documents and photos are collected on the call.

Report and decision

Checks and evidence are put together. A person signs off the final verdict.

Trust and privacy

Evidence you can defend, data you can trust

Consent is taken before recording, location or screen share. Each client sees only its own cases. Every action is logged with who did it and when.

Human sign-off

Automated checks help, but an investigator approves every final verdict.

Retention control

Files are deleted after the period you set, and deletion requests are tracked. Read our privacy policy.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Praman?

Praman is verification and investigation software by SatyaSakshi LLP for any industry that has to check a person, a business, an asset or a claim. The person joins a recorded video call from a WhatsApp link, and the result comes back as an evidence report with instant ID, vehicle, business, bank and court checks.

Who uses Praman?

Banks, NBFCs and fintechs, insurers and TPAs, employers and HR firms, real estate and co-living operators, hospitals, marketplaces and e-commerce, fleet and mobility companies, businesses onboarding vendors and dealers, and government and welfare programmes.

Does the customer need to install an app?

No. The customer opens a WhatsApp link in their phone browser. An Android app is available only when screen sharing is needed.

Which instant checks are available?

PAN, Aadhaar through DigiLocker, Voter ID, driving licence, passport, face match and liveness, RC, e-challan, FASTag, EPFO employment history, GST, Udyam, company and director, bank account and UPI name, credit score with consent, and court records.

Is personal data safe?

Checks run only with consent. Full Aadhaar numbers are never stored, each client sees only its own cases, every action is logged, and files are deleted after the period you set.
