Questions

What does a PIVC call check?

Identity, date of birth and address, the premium and term the customer expects, health disclosure, income and occupation, nominee, and whether anyone promised guaranteed returns or asked for cash.

Is the customer recorded with consent?

Yes. The customer is told the call is recorded and agrees before the camera, microphone and location are used.

Can the call run without an executive?

Yes. An AI call asks the questions in the customer’s language. Unclear answers are flagged, and the customer can ask for a person at any time.

How is a high-value policy handled?

Cases above your set value can be marked for a live video call with an executive in addition to the automated call.
