Questions

Can a claim be investigated without a field visit?

Yes, for most cases. The claimant joins a recorded video call from a WhatsApp link. Location, time, photos, documents and answers are saved to the case. A field visit is added only when the case really needs it.

Which checks help in a motor claim?

RC details of the vehicle, vehicle number from the chassis number, pending e-challans, FASTag history and the driving licence. These run in seconds and are saved with the case.

Does the AI decide the claim?

No. The AI asks questions and raises flags. An investigator reviews the evidence and approves every final verdict.

Which languages are supported?

The call can run in Hindi, English and other Indian languages. Answers are written down and translated into English for the report.
