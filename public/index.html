ℹ️ How these modes work
Full automation (policy verification): the customer gets a WhatsApp link, joins a video call whenever free, and an AI voice agent does the whole verification: tells the customer it is an AI-assisted call, confirms details, takes a face photo and eye-blink check, a full-body photo standing and sitting (each with date, time and lat/long), reads Aadhaar/PAN sent on WhatsApp and asks the verification questions. Term policies get extra questions (sum assured, other policies, family history, medical tests…). The customer can tap "Talk to a person" anytime.
Full automation (motor claim): the AI voice agent confirms the claim and vehicle details, takes a face photo and eye-blink check, reads Aadhaar/PAN sent on WhatsApp, asks about the loss, police/FIR and witnesses, and asks the customer to search their WhatsApp (accident, theft, police, vehicle number) and send screenshots. Use 📹 Live video call in the case to see the vehicle yourself.
If policy/claim value is above ₹10,00,000, self-verification is disabled for that case — only a live executive call is allowed.
